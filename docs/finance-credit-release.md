# Canonical Finance OS cash and credit

The dashboard consumes canonical server financial state. Credit limit and available borrowing capacity never become cash, revenue or funding received. Evidenced zero debt is displayed as zero; missing cash, statement and payment amounts remain unknown. Portal period/date strings preserve missing years and do not replace contractual repayment terms.

Release the matching backend and migrations before considering production verification complete. Evidence remains founder/private and original documents are not application constants. The frontend cannot initiate payments or drawdowns from a scenario.

## Deferred Capital on Tap transaction adapter

When the first genuine KLPS Capital on Tap transaction has cleared, export Standard CSV from Capital on Tap and use that real provider file to design and validate the Capital on Tap transaction adapter.

Capital on Tap exports transactions by clearance date, which may differ from transaction date.

Do not design the provider parser before obtaining the real export.
